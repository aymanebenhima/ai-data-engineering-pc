# Section 1 Lab Walkthrough: AWS Management Console

## Objective
Familiarize yourself with the AWS Management Console, which is the web interface for managing your cloud infrastructure.

## Step 1: Creating an AWS Account
1. Go to [aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com/).
2. Click **Create an AWS Account**.
3. Follow the instructions to create a Free Tier account (you will need an email address and a credit card for verification, though you won't be charged if you stick to the Free Tier).

## Step 2: Walkthrough of the AWS Management Console
1. **Log In:** Sign in to the AWS Management Console as the "Root user".
2. **Search Bar:** At the top of the console, there is a search bar. Try typing "EC2" and clicking on the first result to see the Compute dashboard.
3. **Region Selection:** In the top right corner, click the location name (e.g., N. Virginia or Paris). This is where your physical servers will be located. Always choose a region close to your users!
4. **Billing Dashboard:** Search for "Billing" to see your costs. Since it's a new account, it should be $0.00. Set up a "Billing Alarm" so AWS emails you if you ever spend more than $1.

**Congratulations! You have completed your first walkthrough.**
