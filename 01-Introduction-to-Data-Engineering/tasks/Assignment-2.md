# Assignment 2: Good Data Architecture

## Objective
Apply Conway's Law and the AWS Well-Architected Framework to a business problem.

## Scenario
A bank has a mobile app that allows users to check their balance. Currently, the app's database is hosted on a single server located in London. Users in Japan are complaining that the app takes 10 seconds to load their balance. Also, if the server in London needs maintenance, the app goes completely offline for everyone.

## Your Task
Based on the Principles of Good Data Architecture, answer the following:

1. **Location:** How would you fix the speed issue for users in Japan? 
2. **When your systems fail:** How would you fix the issue where the app goes offline during maintenance?
3. **AWS Well-Architected Framework:** Which two pillars of the framework are currently failing in this bank's architecture? Explain why.
