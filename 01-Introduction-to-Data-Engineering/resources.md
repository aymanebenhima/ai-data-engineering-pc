# Resources for Chapter 1: Introduction to Data Engineering

Here is a curated list of the best resources to help you master the concepts in Part 1. We have included easy-to-understand videos, official documentation, and must-read articles.

## 📺 YouTube Videos & Courses
- **[Data Engineering for Beginners (FreeCodeCamp)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qVWzgGG6mAI)**: A fantastic, visual, and easy-to-understand 3-hour course covering the exact basics we discussed.
- **[AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner Training 2020](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3hLmDS179YE)**: Since we cover AWS EC2, VPC, and the Shared Responsibility model, this video explains AWS basics perfectly for beginners.
- **[What is a Data Pipeline? (IBM Technology)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J3nN3hD7u6o)**: A great whiteboard explanation of how data moves from A to B.

## 📚 Books
- **Fundamentals of Data Engineering** by Joe Reis and Matt Housley (O'Reilly). *This book perfectly maps to our syllabus (Lifecycle and Undercurrents).*
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** by Martin Kleppmann (O'Reilly). *A deeper dive into Data Architecture (Section 3).*

## 🌐 Websites & Articles
- **[AWS Shared Responsibility Model (Official)](https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/shared-responsibility-model/)**: The best visual explanation of who secures what in the cloud.
- **[The Downfall of the Data Engineer](https://maximebeauchemin.medium.com/the-downfall-of-the-data-engineer-5bfb701e5d6b)** - A classic article explaining the evolution of the Data Engineer role.
- **[What is Data Engineering? (IBM)](https://www.ibm.com/topics/data-engineering)** - A very simple, beginner-friendly read.

## 🖼️ Helpful Architecture Diagrams
- **[AWS Architecture Icons](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/icons/)**: Download these to start thinking like a Data Architect and building your own diagrams!
- **[Data Engineering Ecosystem Map](https://github.com/aaronxsu/Data-Engineering-Ecosystem)**: A massive visual map of all the tools in the data space.
